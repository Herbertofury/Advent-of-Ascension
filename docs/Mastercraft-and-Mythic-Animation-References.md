- **Mecha Drake / Draconic Forge:** a coherent creature-themed Mastercraft package with related animated presentation. The [official Season 1 page](https://www.callofduty.com/en/blog/blackops6/season-01) identifies the blueprint and associated cosmetics. Study creature personality and material cohesion; do not claim a specific inspection sequence from this announcement alone.
- **Gumball Ballistics and Zaptastic / iDEAD:** the [official bundle listing](https://www.callofduty.com/no/store/bundles/3568) identifies its two Mastercraft blueprints. This is a useful playful-object/weapon reference family; actual inspection timing still needs footage review.

Useful for readable progression and preserving identity across attachments.
- **AS VAL — Double Edge:** the [official article](https://www.callofduty.com/it/blog/2021/03/Call-of-Duty-Mobile-Mythic-Weapon-Drop-Intel) describes a changing electric presentation and selectable defeat effects. Useful for distinct cosmetic effect channels.

Useful inspiration for tiny animated AoA realm scenes, with strict readability and performance budgets.

These examples are a verified starting set, not a completed list of every Mastercraft/Mythic weapon or a claim that each appears in the supplied videos.

## Turn an inspect into a short character moment

For each original AoA weapon, storyboard a readable sequence: **invitation → reveal → interaction → payoff → safe recovery**. Mix restrained everyday inspections with rarer elaborate flourishes. Keep a quick cancel path and a short inspection option. Do not let a long cinematic delay required combat input or leave props/arms stranded after cancellation.

Proposed original AoA examples:

- **Soul Storm:** its default Soul Flame leans toward the player's palm, a small wisp circles the chamber, the fitted disc emitters pulse in sequence, and the wisp settles back into the flame. Candle and Gem variants get appropriate interactions instead of showing a missing flame mount.
- **Baronator:** a miniature spectral sea appears within an optional inspection ornament, followed by a tiny upper/lower broadside salute. This cosmetic salute cannot fire damaging projectiles, consume ammunition or advance gameplay barrel parity.
- **Minigun:** a brief controlled rotor inspection shows orderly motion and a tiny industrial-core diagnostic. Hands stay away from moving barrel geometry, and canceling returns the same firing state.
- **Soul Drainer:** a playful captured wisp approaches the golden ring, slips between the claw-like emitters and returns. The flourish cannot heal the player or affect a target.
- **Abominator:** purple inlays form a temporary rune puzzle along its silver stripe, then resolve into the installed emblem. Preserve the actual installed barrel and charm layout.

These are our design proposals, not descriptions of the COD videos.

## Inspection and customization QA

- Validate start, loop/hold, normal end and cancellation at every major beat, including weapon swap, attack, damage interruption, sprint, death and disconnect.
- Distinguish a weapon inspect from a target finisher and a defeat effect; each has different eligibility and ownership rules.
- Preserve actual ammunition/Spirit, cooldown, attachments and authoritative damage while performing cosmetic flourishes.
- Test all barrel, sight, charm and decal combinations. Missing props must fail visibly in authoring QA, not disappear silently in release.
- Keep stable aim references, bounded lights/particles and adjustable flash/motion. Reuse loaded resources instead of rescanning on every inspect.
- Preserve the requested meaningful visual changes for functional parts. The [official BO6 attachment-skin notes](https://www.callofduty.com/uk/en/patchnotes/2025/03/call-of-duty-black-ops-6-season-03-patch-notes) describe cosmetic look overrides; if we offer similar overrides, the UI must still accurately expose functional differences.
