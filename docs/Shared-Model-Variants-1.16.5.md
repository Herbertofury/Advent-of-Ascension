# Shared models, distinct identities · 1.16.5

Seventeen source-mapped texture variants close gaps that filename-only matching could not resolve: six Gemtraps, three Stripefish, two Skippers and six Dryad Sprite material variants. Actual geometry and textures come from the official AoA 1.16.5-3.6.11 JAR; the pinned source explicitly connects each model with its texture.

![Named fish and Dryad Sprite variants](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/main/docs/previews/1.16.5-shared-model-variants.webp)

Models and textures by **Tslat and contributors**. Independently rendered static documentation poses, not new Enderloom gameplay or animation proof. Runtime lighting, movement and special render layers are not reproduced here. Six Dryad appearances are variants of one entity, not six new mobs.
