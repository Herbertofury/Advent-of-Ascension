# Alternate barrels and expressive skins

Owner-approved direction, 18 October 2026. Every weapon should receive creative, identity-specific barrel transformations. These are planned gameplay designs, not implemented or tested adapters.

## Soul Storm transformation family

| Module | What it does | Animation and impact identity | Required gameplay checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spirit Bolt | Fires discrete spirit-plasma bullets | Short emitter pulse, luminous bolt with trailing wisps, brief soul flare | Projectile collision and declared damage/cost per accepted shot |
| Soul Lance | Focused continuous soul beam | Focusing rings converge; stable narrow beam and restrained contact glow | Server damage interval independent of rendered frames; break on occlusion; stop costs and damage on release |
| Revenant Rotor | Circular rotating disc emitters replace the vertical stack | Indexing rotor, one active aperture per scheduled discharge, flat spinning soul discs | Muzzle index and phase agree with accepted shots; optional deliberate volley is separately declared |
| Wraith Coil | Soul-lightning discharge | Forked spectral arc emitters and branching cyan lightning | Explicit chain count/range, line-of-sight rules, unique targets per pulse and bounded effect budget |
| Disc Choir | Four stacked emitters alternate top to bottom; five, six and further upgrades | Each slot pulses as its flat disc leaves; optional increased output is functional | Actual assembly count, projectile count, schedule and resource policy match |
| Echo Bloom | Expanding spectral ring wave | Petal resonator opens, broad ghostly ring and readable impact ripple | Defined wave collision shape, one eligible hit per target per pulse, declared terrain policy |

The original released weapon remains documented separately: its inspected renderer produces cyan/blue sparkler clusters. Discs, beams, lightning and these alternate barrels are intentional new modes, not claims about the original.

## Tailor ideas to each weapon

- **Baronator:** Broadside twin-barrel alternating fire; Thunderbell spectral shockwave muzzle; Siege Comet heavy fantasy projectile; Emberwake furnace-styled flame-energy attachment; optional simultaneous Royal Salvo with explicitly increased output. Preserve its red/graphite naval mass and distinguish original bonus grenades from new modes.
- **Minigun:** Stormwheel rapid rotary fire; Prism Spindle energy tracer conversion; Frost Chorus chilling pulse stream; Spectral Loom soul-disc conversion; Scatter Crown deliberately dispersed volleys. Preserve orange industrial identity and visible rotary behavior; each conversion declares its resource and effect adapter.
- **Every remaining weapon:** derive variants from the actual source weapon, its realm, projectile, special effect and silhouette. Do not stamp the same six barrels onto the entire arsenal. Each profile gets a readable purpose, original evidence, proposed transformations and acceptance status.

## Expressive skins

Use Fallout 4 as modular customization inspiration and COD, Black Ops and Modern Warfare as presentation inspiration, without representing their assets as ours. Create original reactive finishes, animated rune inlays, wear tiers, realm-themed materials and progression skins. Examples: Soul Storm's Ghostglass, Eclipse Reliquary and Lelyetian Spiritbloom; Baronator's Crimson Admiral and Ashen Dreadnought; Minigun's Industrial Sentinel and Neon Foundry.

An explicitly cosmetic finish does not secretly alter statistics. A functional conversion has a named module, accurate tooltip and matching behavior. Reactive skins can respond to firing, accepted hits, charging and streaks without hiding targets or creating uncontrolled lights. Include reduced-flash and reduced-particle settings.

## Preserve the entire concept history

Never delete or overwrite prior concept art. Save new iterations with new versioned names, source/provenance, purpose and known issues. Mark superseded concepts clearly while keeping them browsable. Rejected art can still contain a useful idea.

## Actual original flight and impact capture milestone

Capture the original weapon in the real target-version game, then the upgraded build under the same conditions. Record exact Minecraft/AoA versions, other mods, shaders, resource packs, graphics settings, camera/FOV and whether footage is normal speed or slowed. Capture launch, mid-flight, entity impact, block impact and any secondary effect separately. Include screenshots and short clips with the original item and projectile visible. Keep source-extracted textures and generated concepts labeled separately from actual game captures. No in-game capture has been completed by this concept sheet.
