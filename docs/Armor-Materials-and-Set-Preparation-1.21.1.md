## Prepare before the encounter

1. Match the release shown above to your installation. A familiar item name does not establish identical properties in an older release.
2. Pick a set below and open its four item cards. Each card links the matching helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots, plus exact-item recipe and mob-drop searches. An empty recipe/drop result does not prove the item is unobtainable: another mechanic, trader or a different build can supply it.
3. Read the intended special effects separately. Base armor and toughness are inputs, not a damage-block percentage. Material totals alone cannot rank fall protection, ranged bonuses, resource effects or encounter-specific utility.
4. Check the real worn slots and applicable equipment requirements. Eleven special headpieces declare compatibility with any set, but that does not waive the other pieces or guarantee every special effect on every modpack.
5. Check remaining condition and your repair method before leaving. This release's inspected material builders use an empty normal repair ingredient; that is not a blanket prohibition on Mending, custom recipes or other repair systems. See the separate [Mending Table guide](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/wiki/Mending-Table-and-Repair-Materials-1.21.1).
6. Test the complete loadout in a safe place before a boss. Difficulty, damage type, attributes, enchantments, equipment checks, active data and other mods can change the result.

## What the item-card numbers mean

- **Base armor:** the material's slot-specific input. Do not read “5” as “5% damage reduction.”
- **Toughness:** the per-piece material input, not a whole-set total or a universal resistance percentage.
- **Knockback resistance:** all 55 inspected material builders leave this material input at zero. Separate special attributes and effects can still matter.
- **Enchantability:** normally 10 here; Skill Helmet material sets 20, Old Boot sets 0. Eligibility and behavior can be overridden separately.
- **Durability reference:** the inspected official vanilla slot formula multiplies the AoA constructor's base multiplier by 11 for a helmet, 16 for a chestplate, 15 for leggings, or 13 for boots. Cards label this as a vanilla-formula reference, not a measured live-modpack guarantee.
- **Skill helmets:** all six add an unbreakable component and override `getMaxDamage` to return 0. The hidden base calculation is not ordinary wear capacity. The cards call this out instead of advertising a misleading 550-use lifetime.

## When a set seems not to work

- Check every occupied armor slot and the exact item IDs. Similar colors or names do not establish the same material.
- Check whether the bonus is per piece, full-set only, conditional on damage type, or handled by another event. This base-property table intentionally does not invent a complete special-effect summary.
- Check skill/equipment requirements and the active build. Creative behavior is not a substitute for a Survival test.
- If the card shows no recipe, follow its drop search and the [trading guides](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/wiki/Trading-and-Traders-1.21.1), then inspect the build's acquisition data. Do not copy an old-release recipe into a modern page without evidence.
- If an armor panel is absent on an older-release card, that means this modern evidence was not substituted into it.
