# Weapon origin data index · AoA 3.7.1

This is a research starting point for lore-preserving redesigns, not a completed lore encyclopedia.

The pinned Minecraft 1.20.1 archive contains references for 228 first-target weapon IDs: 164 have a packaged recipe, 65 have an upgrade-kit recipe, and 18 appear directly in an item loot-table entry. These categories overlap. Missing references do not establish unobtainability: code, trades, other data formats and historical versions require separate review.

## Useful starting leads

- Apoco Assault Rifle: Mechanical Assault Rifle + Apoco Upgrade Kit. The kit occurs in the packaged Vox Ponds world table. Apoco Rifle is a different weapon, upgraded from Discharge Rifle.
- Iominator: Abominator + Ancient Upgrade Kit; the kit appears in packaged Shyrelands structure loot. This does not establish a named inventor or the physical place of manufacture.
- Soul Storm: its infusion recipe references Blaster Frame, Ghoulasm, Ghostly Powder and the Ghastly ingot tag. These are stronger design leads than inventing generic technological ancestry.
- Baronator: its infusion recipe references Gun Frame, Grenade and Baronyte/Iron/Varsium ingot tags. Material origins need their own research before assigning geographical lore.
- Discharge Shotgun: no recipe/direct item loot reference found in these scanned categories. Keep its origin unresolved instead of borrowing Discharge Rifle lore.

## Evidence boundaries

Each entry carries its archive path and SHA-256. The archive hash is pinned in the report and generator. Item/tag lists flatten references; they are not ingredient counts or a claim that all alternatives must be consumed. Loot references do not calculate chances or evaluate conditions. A world path is a data association, not proof that a dimension is active in this target release. Every row starts with lore review incomplete.

Creator intent, maker attribution, original material meaning, historical changes and native availability require additional evidence. See [Weapon Lore and Provenance](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/wiki/Weapon-Lore-and-Provenance).
