## What the references establish

- [Rock Island Auction's Guycot rifle specimen description](https://www.rockislandauction.com/detail/48/1074/rare-french-guycot-chain-rifle), December 4, 2009: the historical system includes chain indexing and a distinct firing-pin action. A moving chain alone does not explain its firing cycle. The specimen description is historical evidence, not a tested reconstruction.
- [First-hand Guycot study and author photograph](https://blog.coredump.cx/p/random-objects-guycot-and-jarre), June 16, 2024: the author shows a display window and describes trigger-driven indexing followed by firing-pin action. The visible chain is only part of the system.
- [BattleFalcon BF-AR16a product description](https://battlefalconstore.com/product/bf-ar16a/), reviewed October 27, 2026: identifies a repeating ball-feed compound bow, cams, string and dry-fire protection. It does not establish that our proposed gun-trigger, self-resetting fantasy conversion already exists. Manufacturer performance claims are not independently verified or imported as game stats.

Only high-level roles and silhouette cues are used. Reference photos are not redistributed. No real dimensions, pressures, linkage design, machining or conversion instructions are part of this proposal.

## Runewinder correction: explicit fictional actuation

The next approved concept must show a separate **rune-striker actuator** and its return indicator, not just a necklace floating in a window. This is an invented magical activation component, not a real cartridge ignition assembly. Name its model/animation parts and visibly distinguish it from the bead indexer and output emitter. The striker addresses the aligned glyph through a magical coupling; beads are never physically destroyed or ejected.

The game animation contract must represent ready, authorized activation, spent-bead darkening, index advance and reset. Only an authoritative accepted primary-shot event may commit charge/cursor changes. Cosmetic trigger movement alone grants no projectile, bead consumption or mana debit. Show the full return portion of the closed necklace and reserve actual volume for the actuator, grip and rotating sleeve. Neither a cutaway drawing nor this text closes the rig/native gate.

## New archergun: a different release system

The BF-AR16a-inspired weapon needs actual readable limbs, two cams and a continuous string silhouette, not decorative wings on a rifle. Keep the string and projectile presentation clear of the glass necklace, trigger hand and support hand.

Its fictional gun-style trigger requests a **rune release gate**; a separately visible **mana draw-drive** explains the fantasy reset. A bow is not automatically improved by copying a firearm striker into it. The concept must distinguish these visual components from the necklace indexer. State the mana source and budget for reset in gameplay design rather than depicting perpetual free mechanical work. Exact costs and timings are unbalanced and unimplemented.

The bead remains attached and darkens after an accepted shot, preserving its element, identity and position in the ordered necklace. The arrow-like energy shape in the first draft is provisional, not an approved replacement for the project's physical-arrow archergun convention. Resolve projectile ownership, staging and the relationship between arrow supply and bead charge before native implementation. Do not claim a floating glow proves contact with the string.
