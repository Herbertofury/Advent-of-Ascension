# Soul Storm · lore and material provenance

**Pinned release: AoA 3.7.1, Minecraft 1.20.1. Source evidence, not native acceptance.**

## Confirmed recipe identity

The packaged infusion recipe combines a Blaster Frame with two Ghoulasm entries, three Ghostly Powder entries and one Ghastly ingot-tag entry to produce Soul Storm. The Ghoulasm occurs in the packaged Greckon world loot table. The scan found seven ingredient-related loot tables in total. Ghostly Powder appears in spectral creature entries such as Ghost, Apparition and Banshee. These references establish material associations, not a named inventor or proof that every historical world is playable in this release.

Soul Storm's baseline remains a Spirit-powered blaster with its documented original projectile behavior. Neither spectral ingredients nor the proposed plasma-disc visuals permit replacing its original resource with bullets or inventing poison/explosion behavior.

## Design implications and limits

- Preserve haunted/spectral material identity, the original indigo/cyan palette and recognizable emitter silhouette.
- The user-directed animated soul-flame sight, optional candle/gem sights and alternate plasma emitters are our upgrade designs. Label them accordingly.
- New engraved casings, spectral conduits or material stories remain proposals unless separately sourced.
- Unknown original maker, construction history and artist-intended symbolism stay unknown.
- Check recipe availability and actual gameplay independently; retained files alone cannot pass either.
