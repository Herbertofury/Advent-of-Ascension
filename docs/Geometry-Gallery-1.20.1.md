# Geometry galleries · 1.20.1

**109 official artifact model/texture pairs.** Static geometry showcases, including creatures, fish and other entity resources. These are not in-game tests. Models and textures by Tslat and contributors. Geometry and textures come from the same release JAR. Where a previously rendered pair was byte-identical, its render was reused by SHA-256 instead of recomputed.

Animations, runtime scaling, particles and custom shader effects are not represented. Missing texture variants are tracked separately. A resource name is not proof of a distinct living mob registry entry.
