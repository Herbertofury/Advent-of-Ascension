## Coverage and limits

The release scan found exactly 70 recipes of type `aoa3:upgrade_kit`; every one appears here once. All 210 ingredient/output illustrations resolved to actual released textures. Animated strips use their first declared frame; they are not animated previews. Recipe weights, damage, or an upgrade kit’s name are not used to invent effects or lore. This atlas includes bows, staves and melee weapons as well as firearms.
