## Distinctions preserved

Classics Reborn, ARIPS, Forgotten Guns, Lavender WWI, Relics & Antiques, BlueArchive FPS JP, Warsaw, Disassembly, Nakara, Craftsman Gunblade and Originium Weapons are among the substantive gaps found. Experimental and historical references remain distinct from polished current-release recommendations.

TWX Laboratory's public trial is not its paid full edition. Meteorite Technology APX distinguishes the public file from supporter features. Delta Force's public version is free while its creator limits paid beta access regionally. Menophage explicitly does not support TaCZ 1.1.4 or newer; the ScoredCupid standalone AK-12 is discontinued. Exact file dates and creator warnings remain visible.

Koei's Unique Armaments has an authenticated public creator identity, release announcement and named firearm-showcase episodes. Its exact current downloadable file, price/access and Minecraft loader/version pair remain unknown. Video views and likes are not gunpack download or favorite metrics.

R3 Carbine has an exact public v1.0.0 firearm ZIP release dated October 22, 2026. Its creator discloses AI-assisted model/texture work and high-polygon/TML requirements. The catalog labels these creator renders and experimental status; this task generated no images and does not adopt the creator's runtime/performance claims. Separate ammo/editor assets are not counted as guns or popularity evidence.
