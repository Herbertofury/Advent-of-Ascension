## Qualified result

This second gap-directed sweep adds seven distinct creator/source families to the existing 206-family TaCZ collection. The parent atlas grows from 2,410 to 2,417 records; TaCZ grows to 213 families; the editorial quality shortlist gains four entries, becoming 58.

The additions are THE HARBINGER, Sentinel Technology / STF, Team Fortress 2's Gun / TFG, Extreme Armaments / Ningmeng Craft, Dragon Technology, TRW Low Maintenance Rifle and XColorful's TaCZ-PUBG. Engineering tests and discontinued source snapshots are explicitly separated from current stable-file recommendations. The same creator's MPX and earlier MK14 lineage is conservatively grouped rather than falsely claimed to be one combined current ZIP.

All 21 source previews/icons and all seven card selections were visually inspected. Harbinger uses actual firearm artwork rather than its logo-only latest-update cover. Source inventory icons are labeled as icons, not gameplay screenshots. No image generation was used.

## Research coverage

The completed provider sweep records 3,206 distinct search listings, 32 terminal query traversals, 802 candidate metadata/source snapshots, ten focused repository manifest/release checks and 37 exact creator-video metadata records. The queries span broader gun/weapon/armory/Arcana terms, different CurseForge project categories, Modrinth, GitHub, Chinese/Japanese names and public original-creator distribution references. Four provider-rate-limited search tails were resumed at reduced pressure, without repeating completed pages.

The large search counts include irrelevant keyword collisions, utilities, other engines, base-runtime forks, server/modpack redistribution and existing projects. They are not counts of thousands of actual TaCZ gunpacks. The source decision ledger preserves unqualified and inaccessible leads rather than asserting that they do not exist. This is a broad finite search, not a provable census of every private or unlisted creator pack. The final ledger retains 144 unresolved source leads and 19 source-access limitations; these are not silently promoted into verified pack recommendations.

The challenge pass also checked potentially misleading repository names. Hexcraft Workshop, MerrySnow's TaCZ addon, Crisp, Create-TACZ-Guns and taczadd describe effects, workbenches, handling, recipes or upgrades rather than a separately verified new firearm roster. KevinWu's PUBG workspace explicitly omits visual/gun-library inputs from its public repository. ResonancesGunpacks requires PointBlank Jelly, not TaCZ. Soul Shooter is a separate HTML/Godot game. These are not promoted merely because their names contain gun or TaCZ.

## Honest source distinctions

- Harbinger has a creator-announced v0.0.4 beta. Earlier releases are explicitly free, but current file/access details remain unconfirmed. Canceled ESSENCE is not counted as a second current successor pack.
- Sentinel Technology is a creator-hosted engineering-test edition; its public Afdian destination is verified, not its current price or exact downloadable-file compatibility.
- TFG has a creator-announced v2.1.5 and an author-maintained free-project page. Planned weapons are not counted as released content.
- Extreme Armaments has an MPX release announcement and an earlier creator-linked MK14 share. Its unfinished AX50 preview is excluded from released content.
- Dragon Technology's original v0.0.2 creator release supplies the public download. A later GitHub tag without uploaded assets is not treated as a new packaged release.
- LMR has an exact public lmr-1.0.0.zip asset and a creator-stated TaCZ 1.1.4+ requirement, but no independently established Minecraft/loader pair. Its exact asset's download count is kept distinct from repository stars.
- XColorful's PUBG pack has a public namespace manifest and Beryl M762, Groza and SLR source indexes. The author discontinued it; no tagged playable release is invented.

Video views, video likes, creator followers and GitHub stars are not converted into gunpack downloads, favorites or community ratings. No paid attachments, mod binaries or video streams were retrieved. No native Minecraft compatibility or performance testing is claimed.
