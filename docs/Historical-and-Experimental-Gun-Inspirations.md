# Historical & Experimental Gun Inspirations

**AoA armory concept board | October 22, 2026 | Research and inspiration, not implemented content.**

[Weapon Overhaul](Weapon-Overhaul) · [Weapon Reference Library](Weapon-Reference-Library) · [Weapon Upgrade Backlog](Weapon-Upgrade-Backlog) · [Attachment Concept Gallery](Attachment-Concept-Gallery)

A visual-first encyclopedia of unusual guns and gun-like historical weapon systems for **Advent of Ascension**. Focus on weird feeds, gorgeous silhouettes, interesting motions, satisfying animations, rare mechanisms, fantasy remix potential, and strong design identity. These are creative references rather than tutorials or mechanical construction instructions.

## Image and video gallery usage

Every entry has a **📷 real-photo gallery** linking to Wikimedia Commons image results, a **🔍 history reference search**, and a **▶ demonstration search**. These are real image/video discovery links, not fabricated renders or claims that particular videos have been checked. Embed a specific Commons thumbnail only after the exact file and license have been verified. This keeps the research catalog accurate even when an obscure historic gun has no stable freely usable photo.

## AoA design/production rules

- Preserve the recognizable original AoA model and core projectile/resource gameplay, with original and premium 3D presentation as separate reversible options.
- For each selected reference design a distinct draw, idle, inspect, fire/recovery, reload (if enabled), interruption, ground/third-person pose, sound, and impact presentation.
- Do not equate fantasy cosmetics with altered damage, firing patterns, energy costs or reload behavior unless an explicitly chosen gameplay upgrade has separate implementation and testing.
- For every approved photo, record its exact Wikimedia file URL, uploader/attribution, license and availability; do not confuse Commons image search with a verified licensed image.
- For every selected video, verify a direct watch URL and what firing/mechanism details it actually demonstrates.
- Finish source/model comparison, hand fit, sight alignment, animated collision clearance, native client/server integration, save behavior, fallback, and measured frame-time validation before checking off production.
