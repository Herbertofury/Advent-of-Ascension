# Entity atlas · 1.12.2

**467 mapped model/texture presentations across 447 source entity IDs.** Counts include texture variants and non-living entity resources. These are additional generation-specific references, not a count of distinct mobs added to Enderloom.

The literal importer converted 411 of 415 inspected model classes after adding explicit ModelBox support. Renderer registrations establish source IDs and shared/variant texture mappings. Missing inherited models and unresolved renderers remain open.
