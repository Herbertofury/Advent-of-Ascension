What the numbers mean

Extra healing uses the selected food’s maximum healing × min(missing hunger ÷ food hunger, 1), with pre-meal hunger. Saturation uses the inspected vanilla comparison: 2 × the food’s hunger value × modifier, capped by the resulting hunger level.

Inputs are bounded to the normal 0–20 hunger model. Saturation above current hunger is clamped and flagged. A full-hunger result is an explanatory boundary, not permission to eat when the normal use gate refuses it. Missing or modified food components and modded hunger systems need separate validation.
