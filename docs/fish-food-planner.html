Healing is reduced by how much hunger was missing before eating. Actual healing is capped by missing health and may be changed by Forge hooks. The helper skips dead, zero-health or already-full-health entities. Raw fish do not have the special HealingFood callback.

Why full healing is not guaranteed

The cooked fish callback multiplies its configured healing by min(missing hunger ÷ food hunger, 1), using the pre-meal hunger value. It then runs ordinary food consumption before asking the healing helper to heal. The saturation modifier is not the amount restored: the vanilla comparison requests 2 × hunger value × modifier, capped by the resulting hunger level.

Inputs are bounded to the normal 0–20 hunger model. Saturation above current hunger is clamped and flagged. A computed full-hunger result is an explanatory boundary, not permission to eat when the normal use gate refuses it. Modded hunger systems and altered food hooks require separate validation.
