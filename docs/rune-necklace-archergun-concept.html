Runewinder Archergun

One necklace family. Two explicitly different firing configurations.

Base · Arrow through barrel

The bowstring propels the arrow through the barrel, assisted by the fictional rune striker activating a bead. Only the arrow is fired. A matching base concept and rig are still required.

Upgrade · Two paths, same moment

The arrow fires from the recessed top channel while a separate magical projectile fires through the clear barrel at the same time. Not either/or, and no waiting for the arrow to clear.

Internals remain an explicit gate

Striker, release/reset, bead indexing, string/arrow seating, costs and synchronized native output must be authored and tested. Two upgrade effects must not accidentally double-advance the necklace.

Complete proposal and open tests

Generated concept candidates, not in-game. No native/mechanical acceptance. No scripts or autoplay.
