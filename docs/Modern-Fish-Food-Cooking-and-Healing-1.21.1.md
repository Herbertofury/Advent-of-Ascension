# Modern fish food, cooking and healing · 1.21.1

**Minecraft 1.21.1 · AoA 3.7.16.1.** This page checks 27 registered fish-related foods: the 24 entries supplied by AoA’s raw-fish tag, Skelecanth, Cooked Fish and Cooked Rainbowfish. Similar names do not mean the same food behavior as [AoA 3.3.6 for 1.12.2](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/wiki/Cooking-Fish-and-Hunger-Scaled-Healing-1.12.2).

![Three distinct modern fish effect requests](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/main/docs/previews/modern-fish-effect-comparison-1.21.1.webp)

[Calculate a modern fish meal](https://herbertofury.github.io/Advent-of-Ascension/fish-food-planner.html?v=1.21.1). Switch releases to compare the documented rules, or share a link with your chosen fish and input values.

## The important changes

- **Twenty fish converge on one Cooked Fish item.** It provides 6 nutrition with a 0.85 saturation modifier and requests Conduit Power for 600 ticks. Its registered class is ordinary Item; it does not use AoA’s HealingFood callback.
- **Cooked Rainbowfish stays separate.** It has the same nutrition/modifier, requests Luck for 600 ticks, and uses the HealingFood subclass with configured maximum extra healing of **5 health points**, scaled by pre-meal missing hunger.
- **Jamfish is a special raw food.** It provides 5 nutrition, a 0.5 saturation modifier and a Speed request for 600 ticks. Its custom class adds tooltip text, not the cooked-Rainbowfish healing callback.
- **Skelecanth is edible in the checked registration**, with 1 nutrition and a 0.1 modifier, but is absent from this release’s supplied raw-fish tag. Do not infer tag membership solely from a fish-like name.

The three effect suppliers use declared probability 1. Their bundled EffectBuilder defaults produce amplifier 0, ambient false, particles shown and icon shown. This describes the supplied requests; existing effects and NeoForge/mod hooks can still affect actual application. Six hundred ticks is nominally 30 seconds at 20 TPS.

## Cooked Rainbowfish: the healing calculation

The registered constructor passes healing amount 5. HealingFood samples hunger before normal item consumption and requests:

**5 × min((20 − hunger before eating) ÷ 6, 1)** health points.

| Hunger before eating | Extra healing request | Hearts equivalent |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 14 or less | 5 | 2.5 |
| 17 | 2.5 | 1.25 |
| 19 | about 0.8333 | about 0.4167 |
| 20 | 0 | 0; normal eating eligibility still applies |

Actual healing is limited by missing health and helper/hook behavior. The 21 bounded arithmetic cases are not a running-game test. Food components can be modified; a restoration should validate missing or zero-nutrition food properties rather than assume every transformed stack retains the original component.

## Do not copy the historical values

| Comparison | 1.12.2 / AoA 3.3.6 | 1.21.1 / AoA 3.7.16.1 |
|---|---|---|
| Candlefish identity | Raw Candlefish and a distinct cooked Candlefish | Candlefish input, generic Cooked Fish output |
| Cooked fish healing | Sixteen species use their own HealingFishFood values | Generic Cooked Fish has no AoA HealingFood callback |
| Cooked Rainbowfish | Historical Rainbowfish: 9 hunger, maximum healing 10 | Cooked Rainbowfish: 6 nutrition, maximum healing 5, plus Luck request |
| Nearly full Rainbowfish meal, hunger 19 | About 1.1111 extra health points | About 0.8333 extra health points |

These are two pinned releases, not a claim about every intermediate version. Use the [version-aware food planner](https://herbertofury.github.io/Advent-of-Ascension/fish-food-planner.html?v=1.21.1) with the release selector set correctly. It keeps 1.12.2 and 1.21.1 datasets separate and rejects unindexed releases or mismatched fish links.
