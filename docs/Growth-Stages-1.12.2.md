# Historical growth stages · 1.12.2

**128 age-variant presentations across 16 plant blockstate records.** Every texture and model comes from this exact release’s official artifact. These are documented age states, not a statement about harvest rules, maturity thresholds, growth timing or biome/light conditions.

[Modern growth guide](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/wiki/Crops-and-Growth-Stages) · [Historical galleries](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/wiki/Historical-Galleries)

The legacy blockstate model references are resolved under `models/block/` before reading their parent models. Each resolved path was checked in the artifact. [Forge’s blockstate documentation](https://docs.minecraftforge.net/en/1.12.x/models/blockstates/introduction/) explains the variant-to-model relationship. Older names and appearances are preserved rather than replaced with newer-release assets.
