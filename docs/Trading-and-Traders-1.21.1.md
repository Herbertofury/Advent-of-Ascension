# Trading and Traders · 1.21.1

**Minecraft 1.21.1 / AoA 3.7.16.1.** This handbook catalogs **55 base offer definitions**: seventeen fixed AoA trader offers, 22 dimension-conditional Herald offers and 16 additions to villager profession pools. It is not a promise that one trader offers all of them, that every retained dimension is playable, or that final displayed prices match the base input.

[Where modern traders spawn, including Precasian hovels](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/wiki/Finding-Traders-1.21.1)

[Open the searchable Trading Desk](https://herbertofury.github.io/Advent-of-Ascension/trade-explorer.html?v=1.21.1) · [Historical 1.16.5 traders](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/wiki/Trading-and-Traders-1.16.5)

## Before you spend your coins

- **Base prices can change.** Reputation, demand and other runtime/modpack changes are not evaluated here. Inspect the price in the actual trade screen.
- **Max uses means transactions before restocking**, not total output items. A three-item output used 16 times represents 48 items before other changes.
- **Merchant XP is profession progression**, not the number of player experience orbs. The AoA trader class calculates player XP separately.
- Profession rows are **candidates added to their level pools**. A particular villager may not select every listed offer.
- Bring the listed coin denomination. This extraction does not establish a universal coin-conversion recipe. [Find Copper Coin loot references](https://herbertofury.github.io/Advent-of-Ascension/drop-finder.html?v=1.21.1&q=aoa3%3Acopper_coin) and [Silver Coin references](https://herbertofury.github.io/Advent-of-Ascension/drop-finder.html?v=1.21.1&q=aoa3%3Asilver_coin).

## Why a Lottoman or Herald may not restock

The custom `TraderRestockGoal` requires all of the following before its start logic can attempt a restock:

1. The trader is alive and is not currently trading.
2. The inherited `canRestock()` and `allowedToRestock()` checks pass.
3. The world finds no nearby alive player within the **24-block radius** used by the goal.
4. The start step still checks `shouldRestock()` before calling `restock()`.

Practical troubleshooting: close the trade screen and step more than 24 blocks away; another nearby player may still prevent the goal from running. This is a necessary condition, not a fixed restock timer. The rule belongs to the custom AoA trader goal and should not be applied wholesale to ordinary Clerics, Butchers, Cartographers or Assassin-profession villagers.

## Merchant XP versus player XP

The offer’s XP value contributes to merchant progression. For the custom AoA trader class, the player-orb calculation starts with 3–6, may add five on a level-up path, then divides by `maxUses / 16f` with integer assignment. For a 729-use Herald offer, those branches compute zero player-orb XP even though the offer has 20 merchant XP. This is a source calculation, not an in-game XP measurement; other hooks may change behavior.

## The Corrupted Traveller uses a different screen

The Corrupted Traveller is not a normal MerchantOffer trader in this build. It opens its own menu when alive and interacted with without sneaking, after its Name Tag handling. Its food/book exchange is documented in the [beginner walkthrough](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/wiki/Beginner-Walkthrough) and [released exchange evidence](https://herbertofury.github.io/Advent-of-Ascension/traveller-exchange-evidence.json). Do not look for it in the ordinary coin-offer tables above.
