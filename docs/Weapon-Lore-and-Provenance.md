# Weapon lore and provenance

A recognizable redesign preserves the weapon's place in AoA, not only its colors or damage values. Check this before approving its canonical concept, and keep it consistent across Minecraft-fit and concept-faithful presentation.

## Required research for every weapon

- Pin the release and exact item ID. Similar names and later versions may have different origins or behavior.
- Trace acquisition, recipe ingredients, upgrade ancestry, associated dimensions, mobs, factions and materials.
- Record explicit lore and creator statements where available. Identify whose statement it is and retain a source.
- Compare the original sprite/model with its world's plants, creatures, structures and material language.
- Separate confirmed facts, visual interpretations and newly proposed flavor. Unknown makers or construction stories stay unknown.
- Explain which visual landmarks carry that identity into the concept, model, textures, effects, variants and animations.
- Do not infer damage effects from the environment: a toxic setting does not automatically give the weapon poison damage.
- Recheck both visual treatments. The aesthetic switch must preserve the same lore and mechanical identity.

## Apoco: what is confirmed

The pinned AoA 3.7.1 recipe upgrades Mechanical Assault Rifle with Apoco Upgrade Kit into Apoco Assault Rifle. The packaged Vox Ponds world loot table includes that kit. The distinct Apoco Rifle instead upgrades from Discharge Rifle. These are different weapon IDs.

The [official upgrade-kit reference](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Apoco_Upgrade_Kit) also associates the kit with Vox Ponds. Its documented setting combines pollution, fungi, damaged vegetation, ruins and robotic inhabitants. [Vox Ponds reference](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Vox_Ponds)

One step further back, its incomplete base comes from Iro Crates, associated with Iromine; Power Core and Mecha Gear complete the Mechanical Assault Rifle. [Iro Crate reference](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Iro_Crate)

This establishes a mechanical ancestry plus the Vox Ponds upgrade connection. It does not identify an in-world inventor or prove where the upgrade was physically performed. Retained data/assets also do not by themselves establish native dimension availability in a particular target release.

## Apoco: what remains interpretation

Reading the red upper shapes as fungal caps and the lower fittings as roots is a plausible visual interpretation, but the checked sources do not establish the original artist's intent. Preserve that possibility instead of automatically converting every rounded form into a metal machine housing. Clearly label any added fungal growth, root construction or new backstory as our adaptation unless a source confirms it.

The replacement concept must undergo this lore review before model acceptance. Its current mechanical treatment is not exempt merely because its outline is closer than the rejected first design.
