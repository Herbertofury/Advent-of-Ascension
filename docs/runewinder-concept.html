NEW WEAPON PROPOSAL · WORKING NAME

Runewinder

A luminous necklace beneath crystal. A shared chassis, a visible cycling rune-bead loop, and two first directions: standard and purple Soulflame.

Standard · Rune beads

Titanium, graphite and restrained bronze surround ivory/cyan engraved beads. The glass exposes the necklace rather than hiding its movement.

Soulflame · Flame sight

Obsidian and amethyst frame the violet necklace. A small open flame sight forms on focus, with readability and reduced-effects options required.

One family, many dimensions

Future finishes share a coherent rig. Cosmetic swaps must not silently change damage, cadence or resource behavior; functional conversions need separate definitions.

New proposal, not a released AoA item.

The historical Guycot 40 supplies visual inspiration only. Capacity is the installed bead count; ordered bead types select successive projectiles. Spent beads stay attached and go dark, then relight when mana-charged. Exact upgrade limits, mana costs, final name and registry identity remain design work. This proposal is separate from the audited 434 released identities.

Internals are part of the weapon

  • Continuous closed necklace, entirely inside the body.
  • Player-facing glass and visible rune sequencing.
  • One advance per confirmed firing event, not per raw input.
  • Clearance, interruption, save and multiplayer tests.

Build from the best suitable base

Review licensed rigs and rough meshes before rebuilding from zero. Refine the chosen concept, preserve provenance and request author permission where required.

Reuse and quality requirements

Craft the firing order

Arrange ice → fire → ice → fire, poison → fire, purple flame or biome beads. Bead count upgrades change capacity; bead types change projectiles. Cosmetic skins remain separate.

Recharge, don’t discard

Spent beads turn dark but stay on the necklace. Rotate the clear sleeve open, lift the loop into the hand, channel compatible mana, snap it back and rotate the sleeve shut flush.

Corrected reload illustration pending.

The approved access cover is one smooth transparent sleeve rotating around the chamber like a bank-tube carrier. The earlier segmented shutter is rejected and is not shown as the current design.

Rifle conversion · Functional upgrade

A full stock, extended emitter and supporting fore-end convert Runewinder into its rifle configuration. Proposed upgrades target shot effectiveness, useful range, aimed precision and stability; exact values remain subject to balance tests.

Your necklace stays yours

Conversion preserves bead identity, count, element order, charge and next-shot position. It does not grant free rounds or mana. Standard, Soulflame and cyan finishes remain part of the same family.

Concept, implementation and native acceptance are separate. No autoplay or continuous rendering loop. Complete weapon standard
