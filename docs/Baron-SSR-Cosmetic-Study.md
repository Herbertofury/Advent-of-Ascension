# Baron SSR: finish and charm study

2026-10-27 · generated art direction only · native acceptance remains false.

![Three finish directions and detached charm designs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/main/docs/baron-ssr-cosmetics-study-v1.png)

This extends the existing premium Baron SSR concept rather than replacing it. The long dark front, oxblood-red armor, silver receiver, amber optic and enclosed-foot support silhouette remain the design anchors. The lower folded components are supports, not additional muzzles.

## Proposed cosmetic directions

- **Original Fit:** broad red-brown, silver and graphite material blocks. The sheet is a high-fidelity proposal, not a pixel-art texture or a screenshot of the released game. Actual small-scale readability remains untested.
- **Crimson Regalia:** richer lacquer and restrained gold ornament. Keep the silhouette and resource behavior unchanged; detail must survive mipmapping without noisy shimmer.
- **Ashen Relic:** worn silver and dark red ceramic. Wear is a visual treatment, not durability or damage metadata.

Detached heraldic-crest, amber-crystal and red-pennant charms are optional visual concepts. No attachment socket or collision-free motion is established by this sheet. Mounts must be checked against hands, sightline, support deployment and all animation poses. This study introduces no charm gameplay bonuses, acquisition recipes or canonical lore.

## Preserved behavior and acceptance

The current source profile remains authoritative: Metal Slug resource and inherited sniper behavior are not replaced with an invented energy system or physical magazine. Cosmetic selection must not alter timing, projectile count, damage or authoritative firing. Any later functional attachment needs a separately reviewed contract.

The first generated layout cropped part of the third prop. A targeted layout revision restored its full outline; the rejected draft remains local. The final sheet is still a generated approximation, not proof of identical meshes between finishes.
