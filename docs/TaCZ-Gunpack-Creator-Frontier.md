## Search coverage and lookalikes

The provider sweep records **3,206 distinct source listings**, **32 terminal query traversals**, **802 candidate source reads**, **10 focused repository checks**, and **37 exact creator-video metadata records**. Broad results include many unrelated projects and support mods; these counts are not a claim that thousands of TaCZ gunpacks exist.

The canceled ESSENCE predecessor, unfinished Antar and AX50 previews, named-but-unillustrated ZIPs, source templates, base-runtime forks and already-listed creator packs were not padded into the published family count. The source ledger preserves unresolved leads rather than calling them nonexistent.
