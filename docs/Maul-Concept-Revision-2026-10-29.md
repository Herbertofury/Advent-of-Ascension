# Five maul concept revisions · 2026-10-29

These are new art candidates, not finished models or accepted gameplay changes. Earlier artwork remains linked from each gallery entry. All 434 registry identities, original-reference metadata, dimension associations and native/art acceptance flags are preserved; only the five maul presentation entries change. The other 429 entries are unchanged.

## Source-informed visual review

The released AoA 3.7.1 / Minecraft 1.20.1 source-model preview sheets and each previous concept were inspected before generation. Original AoA identity remains credited to Tslat and contributors. These references are not native screenshots, and a source sprite does not establish unseen internal geometry.

The wide canvas fits the complete hero object instead of bending its shaft or cutting off the pommel. This is a visual review of raster silhouettes, not a dimensional proof that all generated views can form one consistent three-dimensional mesh. Not every older image was established to be bent; the new presentation makes the intended straight axis explicit.

## Retained iterations and limits

An initial Coralstone study remained too medieval and was retained rather than promoted. The selected later candidate replaces much of that ornament with modern surfaces. A Vulcammer draft mislabeled two similar detail views as front/rear; the corrected image labels them honestly. Earlier published concepts remain available. [Asset hashes and scoped review receipt](maul-concept-revision-20261029.json).

- Art acceptance, editable geometry, materials/UVs, opposite-view consistency and hand clearance remain open.
- No source combat attributes, recipes, resource costs, effects or registered IDs changed. See the separate [maul behavior review](Maul-Inputs-and-Effects-1.20.1.md).
- Animation ownership, interruption recovery, native rendering, networking and performance still require their own evidence.
- The broader large-melee/greatblade audit is not complete. This pass covers five mauls, not every melee weapon.
- Rune Bead Bow's rejected orientation and Runewinder's rejected reload cover remain separate unresolved tasks. These maul candidates do not silently mark either fixed.

The gallery uses its existing lazy, non-autoplay presentation. Full-resolution candidate images are also used for these five thumbnails, matching the existing revision mechanism; no page-performance improvement is claimed.
