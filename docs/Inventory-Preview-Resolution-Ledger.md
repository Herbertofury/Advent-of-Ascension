# Inventory preview resolution ledger

The initial render reports contain “adapter needed” records that the regular atlas sheets omitted. This page maps all **149 initial exception records** to their current reference or remaining limitation. It prevents completed crop, cloud, sign and bucket supplements from being mistaken for missing work. It also prevents diagnostic pictures from being mistaken for genuine repairs.

These are **version-specific records**, not 149 missing unique items. The original manifests remain initial-pass receipts; this ledger does not rewrite their history.
