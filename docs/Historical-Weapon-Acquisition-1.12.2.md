# Historical weapon acquisition atlas

**Minecraft 1.12.2 · AoA 3.3.6.** Every one of the **412 checked weapon registrations** now has an acquisition-reference panel in the [item explorer](https://herbertofury.github.io/Advent-of-Ascension/inventory-explorer.html?v=1.12.2&family=verified). Search the name, then expand **1.12.2 acquisition references**.

## Follow an actual route

1. **Recipe outputs:** open an exact recipe link, check every input and the workstation type, then follow its weapon usage guide.
2. **Direct loot-table entries:** read the listed table identity. Blocks, reward items, skills, structures and miscellaneous tables are included as well as entities. An entry is not proof that a table is currently invoked by a particular event.
3. **Trader candidates:** inspect the [purchase guide](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/wiki/Buying-Weapons-and-Trader-Upgrades-1.12.2) for quantities, both payment slots and generated versus fixed lists.
4. **No listed route:** read the remaining-gaps section. Code-driven rewards and restoration stations are separate from JSON and literal trader outputs.

### Weights are not percentages

The evidence preserves each complete loot-table definition, its archive path and SHA-256, plus the JSON pointer to every matching item entry. Pool rolls, entry quality, conditions, functions and competing entries can change the result. A weight of 25 does not mean a 25% chance. Nested-table reachability and code-driven bonus drops are not evaluated by this direct-entry scan.

## Evidence, version safety and performance

[Full acquisition evidence](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/main/docs/weapon-acquisition-evidence-1.12.2.json) · [Exact weapon-registration census](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Herbertofury/Advent-of-Ascension/main/docs/historical-weapon-census-1.12.2.json)

The item explorer receives compact references in its existing selected-release data shard. Panel contents are built once, on first expansion; opening them does not trigger another network fetch or scan game files. The full loot definitions are only opened when you choose their evidence link. Later-release cards do not receive historical acquisition advice.

This is a documentation/source-reference audit, not an in-game survival, drop-rate, trading or Enderloom acceptance test. Models and textures are by Tslat and contributors; images reuse the checked source-model portraits.
