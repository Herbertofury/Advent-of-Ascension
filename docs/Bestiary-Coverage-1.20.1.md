## Version fidelity matters

The released **AoA 3.7.1 JAR** and the pinned 1.20 source snapshot are not interchangeable. For example, the released Charger, Veloraptor and Dryad Sprite models still use older resource layouts. The appearance galleries below follow the released classes, not newer source folders that happen to have similar names.

- Charger: ten released geometry/texture pairs, including the default form and nine named variants.
- Veloraptor: brown and green textures on the released shared geometry.
- Dryad Sprite: six released textures on the shared sprite geometry.
- Opteryx: adult model and egg-state model selected by the released `isEgg()` branches.
- Coratee: two intended texture studies. Its released texture getter calls the model-path formatter, so these are restoration references, not proof of working runtime rendering.
- Nine humanoid NPCs: released 64×64 atlas mapping beside a proposed 64×32 correction. The exact textures are 64×32.
- Shiny Squid: pinned-source neutral body-layer geometry with the released texture, explicitly excluding fullbright rendering and animation.

The 26 fish additions and four custom-renderer additions use released initializer/constructor method evidence matched to exact geometry and texture resources. Mesh and texture hashes are retained. The nine humanoid meshes use the official Minecraft 1.20.1 factory, not a lookalike model.
